## Order of a tick

```
Dream may list
  → Field may propose
    → GCAC may emit -1/0/+1   (or hold)
      → M4 may route along one frozen pair
        → Void may ALLOW / HOLD / OVERRIDE
          → Gate-7 commits or quits
            → BUCKET may hit / speak only if the commit earned a bar
```

Hive Pipe can carry any of those messages. It is not in the chain. It is the envelope.

## Ground

Flip is the connected zero of a cell (`1(0)1`).
GCAC `0` is how a write *feels* that zero.
M4 hold is how a hallway *refuses* to walk it.
Void HOLD is how oversight *stops the chain*.

Same family. Four floors. Do not merge the names.

## Clean splits

- Hive Pipe ≠ any of the above
